SikLab.io — Learn, Story, Tutorial, Play edition

The original 26 audio files from the previous All Tutorial Voices project are preserved byte-for-byte: 18 background music/sound effects, 7 custom narrated tutorial pages, and 1 extra voice clip.

Play: Extract the entire ZIP. Keep the audio folder beside index.html. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.

Flow: choose difficulty -> six-scene science story lesson (new) -> seven-page narrated tutorial (original recordings) -> countdown -> two-player snake battle.

NEW STORY AUDIO (optional): Story scenes currently use browser text-to-speech; to add your own narration, place recordings here with these names: lesson-easy-01.mp3 through lesson-easy-06.mp3; lesson-medium-01.mp3 through lesson-medium-06.mp3; lesson-hard-01.mp3 through lesson-hard-06.mp3. Missing story recordings fall back to locally installed speech voices.

Music and effects are in audio/ and the recorded tutorial lives in audio/voice/. Do not rename or remove these files.

Controls: joystick selects scenes; Red/Yellow continues, Green back, White repeat narration, Blue key fact. During a quiz the five buttons answer A–E.
